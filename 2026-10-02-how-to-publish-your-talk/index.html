How to publish your talk

Use the arrow keys or tap the screen to move forward.

1. A single HTML file

Your deck must be self-contained: CSS, JS and images inside the file itself.

That way it works the same here, on your laptop and without wifi.

2. Three tags

The index is generated from your HTML:

  1. <title> with the title
  2. <meta name="author"> with your name
  3. <meta name="description"> with one sentence

3. One folder

Create YYYY-MM-DD-short-title/ at the repo root and save your deck inside as index.html.

With Claude Code: /publish-talk my-talk.html

4. Push

A couple of minutes later you have your link:

acidtango.github.io/acid-talks/YYYY-MM-DD-short-title/

Remember it is public: no client data.
